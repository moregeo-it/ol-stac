--- layout: example.html title: STAC Item with a customized layer style shortdesc: Customizing the style of a COG layer based on STAC metadata. docs: > This example uses the `getLayerOptions` callback to style the single-band near-infrared asset of a STAC Item. The callback derives a grayscale contrast stretch from the scale, offset and data type that the asset reports in its `raster:bands` metadata. The callback can also be asynchronous, e.g. to load a style definition that is referenced in the STAC metadata, which only delays the creation of the individual layer. tags: "stac, stac item, geotiff, cog, style, layer options" ---
