--- layout: example.html title: Cloud Optimized Point Clouds (COPC) shortdesc: Rendering COPC point clouds from STAC Items in a tilted view. docs: > Point clouds in the Cloud Optimized Point Cloud (COPC) format are rendered with WebGL, shaded with eye-dome lighting. With a tilt, the points are shifted towards the top of the screen by their height above the ground (oblique view), so that buildings and trees stand up. Use the controls in the lower left corner to tilt and rotate the view (or Alt+Shift+drag to rotate). By default, the points are colored by their classes if the STAC metadata (classification extension) provides colors for them. The point clouds are from the COPC viewer by Hobu, Inc., from 10 to 364 million points, and from the KyFromAbove STAC API, whose Item describes its reference system as a compound WKT2 with a bound CRS in US survey feet. Only the points needed for the current view are loaded. tags: "stac, stac item, copc, point cloud, lidar, webgl, 3d" ---
